# The queue ratio is only defined when we join a level

claim:      The FIFO queue ratio is degenerate in the regimes where the engine creates a new price level: a joined level starts at ratio 1.0 (level size ahead of us), an empty new level starts at 0.0, and the ratio only becomes informative as our own level accumulates size; therefore the queue-aware gate's measured gain comes from the join regime, and applying it outside that regime adds decisions without information - the join-regime paired net delta is at least 1.5x the all-regime delta.

mechanism:  The paper engine replays FIFO per quote as q_ahead = displayed level size at our price when we join, with arrivals behind us growing q_behind (engine._update_level_queue). The engine prices at mid +- max(0.2 c, 0.75 c) and post-only clamps; with a 1 c tick that places us one tick off mid, which coincides with the best bid/ask only when the spread is about two ticks. In a one-tick book we are one tick behind the touch (an empty new level, ratio 0) and in a three-tick-or-wider book we are alone inside the spread (also a fresh level, ratio 0); in both we are structurally "front of queue" no matter what the flow does, so the 0.60 threshold cannot fire and the gate is inert. Only in the join case does the measured +0.00364 $/share front versus -0.02014 $/share back split apply. The pool pays the same either way, so the distinction is about where protection is real.

data:       DecisionContext only: spread_cents, tick_size, best_bid, best_ask, mid, bid_queue_ratio, ask_queue_ratio, bid_size_at_level, ask_size_at_level. No derived table, no clock.

module:     In decide classify the regime from geometry: join when our reconstructed price equals the touch within the tick (spread of about 2 ticks), lead when it is strictly better than the touch (spread >= 3 ticks), behind when it is worse (spread <= 1 tick). Return the queue-aware rule (keep both at ratio < 0.60, shrink the threatened side above it) only in the join regime; in the lead and behind regimes return QUOTE both at full scale, because the ratio is structurally 0 there and the rule is a no-op that hides the absence of information. Log the regime in the reason string so the ledger counts updates per regime.

metric:     Paired per-condition net against queue_aware_ofi computed separately on the join and non-join subsets, plus the fraction of updates per regime; the deciding number is the join-regime paired delta, with the non-join subset required not to degrade.

kill:       Kill if the join regime is under 10 % of updates, or if the join-regime paired delta is not at least 1.5x the all-regime delta, or if the non-join subset degrades by more than $0.001/share on either slice.

sources:    Queue-position valuation and the FIFO q_ahead/q_behind definition: Moallemi & Yuan (2016), https://moallemi.com/ciamac/papers/queue-value-2016.pdf. Tick size and where quotes sit relative to the touch: "Not Too Big, Not Too Small? Towards an Optimal Tick Size", https://acfr.aut.ac.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/926567/Tick-size-paper.pdf.
