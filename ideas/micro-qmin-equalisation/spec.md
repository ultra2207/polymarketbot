# Overshooting the smaller Q side is a wasted order

claim:      Because Q_min = min(Q1, Q2), every share added to the already-larger Q side earns nothing; after any protective shrink or fill the two Q totals diverge by >= 1.5x in at least 25 % of updates, and equalising them raises rewards_usd by >= 8 % at unchanged fill_notional.

mechanism:  The venue aggregates Q_one = sum of YES bids + NO asks and Q_two = sum of YES asks + NO bids across the market and its complement, then scores Q_min = min(Q1, Q2) inside the band and max(min(Q1,Q2), max(Q1,Q2)/3) inside [0.10, 0.90] (Polymarket docs, eqs. 2-4). Our two tokens quote both sides at the same share size, so the two totals match only while all four orders rest at the same distance; the moment one side is shrunk for protection, or one leg fills, the larger side carries score that is arithmetically discarded. A maker who sizes each side "for safety" therefore buys risk with no reward, and the counterparty fills that overshoot for free. This is the aggregation rule itself - not the single-sided penalty (micro-two-sided-coupling) and not moving a pulled side to the sibling (micro-cross-token-substitution).

data:       DecisionContext for both tokens of the condition (one module instance per condition, decide called per token) plus MarketView.rewards_min_size and rewards_max_spread. The module reconstructs each side's score density from mid/best_bid/best_ask/tick as in micro-score-density-split. No derived table.

module:     Keep, per (asset_id, side), the last computed density f = ((v - s)/v)^2, the last touch prices, and the quote size the harness allocates (max(min_size, capital/mid) reconstructable from the first context's price and the market's capital rule; when unknown, use min_size as the unit). On each decide for token A, compute Q1 = f(A,bid)*size(A,bid) + f(B,ask)*size(B,ask) and Q2 = f(A,ask)*size(A,ask) + f(B,bid)*size(B,bid) from the stored sibling state. If Q1 > 1.2 * Q2, return the Q1-feeding sides at the floor (scale 0.01) and the Q2-feeding sides at full; mirror when Q2 dominates; return QUOTE both when the ratio is inside [0.83, 1.2] or the sibling is unseen. Never PULL here: pulling changes eligibility and would confound the aggregation test.

metric:     Paired per-condition rewards_usd against reactionary at unchanged fill_notional, with the distribution of Q1/Q2 per update and the fraction of updates outside [0.8, 1.25] reported; the deciding number is the paired reward delta per condition. Caveat the run must respect: paper_trading/rewards.compute_epoch_payout sums per-token bid+ask scores with no Q_min and no c factor, so rewards_usd on today's engine cannot express this claim; the run needs the Q1/Q2 proxy from the per-token epoch snapshots or a scoring patch in rewards.py before this metric can falsify anything.

kill:       Kill if Q1/Q2 stays inside [0.8, 1.25] in 90 % or more of updates (nothing to equalise), or if the paired reward gain is under 3 % on both slices, or if fill_notional rises by more than 5 %.

sources:    Polymarket liquidity rewards, Q_one/Q_two aggregation across a market and its complement and the Q_min rule: https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/liquidity-rewards. Two-sided-depth design intent: https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/liquidity. Complementary-leg mechanics used here: src/polymarketbot/rewards/merger.py.
